CodeGroupExplanation
Coverage & limitations

Decodes common international and US groups, visibility bounds, RVR, cloud heights, temperature, pressure, remarks and forecast changes. This is a code explanation tool, not a complete syntax or forecast-validity checker. Dates are displayed as encoded UTC day/time; no month or year is assumed.

Samples are fictional training reports, not current observations. Unrecognized groups remain visible for review. Keep the original report and verify operational information with the official source.
