Variable fuel moment tables

For a tank with a variable arm, enter fuel weight and total fuel moment in the current units, starting with 0 weight / 0 moment. Intermediate moments are linearly interpolated. Tables override fixed tank arms. These are full moments, not moment indexes divided by 100 or 1,000.

% MAC and maneuvering-speed estimate

VA estimate = reference VA × √(takeoff weight / reference weight), only at or below the reference weight. Use the aircraft's published speeds and restrictions. MAC is optional and does not replace an AFM envelope or operating limits.

What this calculator checks

Moment = weight × arm; CG = total moment ÷ total weight. Fuel remaining moments are recomputed at ramp, takeoff, and landing, including entered variable moment tables. Phase weights and CG coordinates are checked against the entered limits. Station and combined-group weight limits are checked separately. The zero-fuel point is informational unless an applicable zero-fuel limit is entered.

This tool supports configurable longitudinal and optional lateral loading models. It does not implement every aircraft's proprietary loading index, fuel-transfer sequence, lateral/longitudinal coupled envelope, floor-pressure limit, or structural loading rule. Transport aircraft and helicopters may require additional approved methods. Aircraft catalogue entries are not proof of full model support.

Save files contain both aircraft profile and current load. Load reads this tool's version 1 JSON format locally; no data is uploaded. No automatic persistence: keep your saved file. Undo can restore the previous replacement or row deletion.
