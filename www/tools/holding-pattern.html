      How to read this calculator

      Direct, parallel and teardrop divide the arrival-heading circle into 180°, 110° and 70° sectors. The left-turn pattern mirrors the right-turn pattern. Within 5° of a dividing line, both adjacent entries are displayed.

      The blue dashed line illustrates entry and the dark racetrack illustrates the established hold. Aircraft-up rotates the view with your arrival heading. Switching inbound/outbound preserves the same physical hold.

      Parallel entry is drawn using the opposite-direction return turn shown in the diagram. Follow the applicable procedure and your flight training. The drawing does not model turn radius, terrain, protected airspace or wind drift through turns.

      Use the aircraft POH/AFM, published hold, applicable speed limits and ATC instructions. This is a planning and training aid, not flight guidance.
